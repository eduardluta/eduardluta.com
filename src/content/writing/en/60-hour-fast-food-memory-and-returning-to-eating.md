After more than 60 hours, the subject of my video changes. I start talking about meals I want to taste again: something from a restaurant, something my wife makes, something my mother prepares.

I am noticing how much affection lives inside food. The memory is not only a flavour. It is a person, their effort and the pleasure of sitting down to something they made.

That observation does not require anyone to fast. You can notice the same care while eating dinner tonight.

## A meal is more than fuel

In the clip, I try to distinguish my wish to enjoy food from physical hunger. That describes how the feeling seemed to me; it is not proof that my body no longer needed nourishment.

The more interesting part is the specific memory. I do not say that I miss an abstract number of calories. I picture someone preparing a dish well. Food carries a relationship: somebody knows what you like, makes time and offers something you can receive.

## Preparing to eat again

The second half of the video is practical. I say that I have handled the end of previous fasts poorly and want to prepare this time. I mention soup and watermelon as my intended foods.

**Those choices are a record of my plan, not a medically checked refeeding protocol.** A particular food does not guarantee safety after prolonged restriction. NICE’s clinical guidance assesses refeeding risk using nutritional history, weight change, blood electrolyte levels and other factors; high-risk patients need skilled professional care.[1]

The number in a video title cannot determine your individual risk. This article neither diagnoses me with refeeding syndrome nor gives a universal safe threshold. If you have been going without food for a prolonged period, especially with illness, medications or previous malnutrition, seek medical advice about returning to eating.

My earlier [24-hour update](/writing/24-hour-water-fast-autophagy-and-evidence/) focuses on claims about the body. Here, the thing worth keeping is simpler: I am thinking about who feeds me and what their care means. The [meal at the end of the series](/writing/ending-a-water-fast-a-meal-is-not-a-parasite-treatment/) brings that social part back into view.

You do not need to earn a meal through deprivation. Appreciation can begin with the food already in front of you.
