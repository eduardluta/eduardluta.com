In this video I am trying to understand the anger in political life. People who can work together at one moment become the centre of hostile camps at another. And ordinary people are invited to carry that hostility into their own relationships.

That is my concern in the clip. I am not presenting a documented case against a named politician. I am asking why disagreement so easily turns into a story in which one person is completely good and another completely bad.

I have never met a person who was only one thing.

## A conviction does not have to become contempt

We can disagree about a decision, a party or a policy. We can demand evidence, challenge a promise and insist on accountability. None of that requires deciding that every person who disagrees with us is an enemy.

In the video I connect this to Albanian solidarity and our sense of a shared future. The point I want to keep is that common interests deserve attention even when we disagree about who should represent them.

Shared interests do not mean automatic agreement or silence about wrongdoing. Unity that cannot tolerate a question becomes another way to avoid scrutiny. I want the question to become more precise, not disappear.

**What happened? What evidence do we have? What would improve the situation?**

## Give the argument something it can solve

Imagine a disagreement over a public service. “Those people ruin everything” tells us who the speaker dislikes. It gives us almost nothing to check.

A more useful version would identify the service, the specific failure, the evidence and the responsible decision. Then it would ask what should change and how we would know whether it had improved. This is an example of a discussion structure, not a report of a particular failure.

The [hospital parking video](/writing/hospital-parking-prishtina-keep-patient-routes-clear/) makes that distinction concrete. A blocked route is something we can inspect. We can discuss who manages it, how guidance could work and whether people can pass. The political argument should help produce an answer to that problem.

The same standard should apply when the decision comes from somebody I like. If my demand for evidence changes with the party label, I am defending a team before I am examining a result.

I do not claim that every dispute is equally reasonable, or that every accusation is false. Some questions require serious investigation and consequences. Precision makes that work stronger. Personal hatred can make it harder to notice when our own side is giving us an empty answer.

Keep the disagreement. Give it a subject worth solving.
