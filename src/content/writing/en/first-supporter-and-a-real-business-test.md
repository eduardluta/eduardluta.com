Reading about Elon Musk brought me to a less distant question: how difficult is it to change yourself? In the video I connect the two. A new company, product or personal direction can look strange before it exists in a form other people understand.

Waiting for everybody to encourage it is a way to wait for a very long time. I suggest finding one person with experience of creating something, someone who can understand the effort as well as the excitement.

The recording also repeats a historical percentage about people rejecting television. Its original survey and wording have not been verified here, so it is not used as evidence for the argument.

## What one supporter can give you

A useful supporter can help sharpen the question, challenge the assumptions and keep you moving when the first attempt is awkward. That person does not have to be famous. Relevant experience and an honest conversation are more useful than status alone.

But support and demand answer different questions. A friend can believe in you without needing your product. A potential customer can have a useful objection without lacking imagination.

The US Small Business Administration’s market-research guidance asks about demand, existing alternatives and what customers pay, and includes direct conversations with consumers as a research method. [1]

## A small example with real arithmetic

Imagine a clearly labelled fictional test: you invite 50 relevant people to discuss a service. Twenty reply, five try it, and two of those five buy. These are invented inputs for explaining the calculation, not results from my business and not target conversion rates.

The reply rate is 20 ÷ 50 = **40%**. Trial participation among respondents is 5 ÷ 20 = **25%**. Purchases among trial users are 2 ÷ 5 = **40%**. Across everyone contacted, the purchase rate is 2 ÷ 50 = **4%**.

The denominator changes the meaning. Saying that 40% bought would be misleading without adding “of the five trial users.” Only two of the original fifty purchased.

This sample is far too small to establish a stable market rate. It does, however, give you something more concrete to discuss than general encouragement. Why did people decline? What did trial users understand? What would buyers return for? Revenue, delivery costs and repeat use would require further evidence.

## Keep belief open to revision

Decide what you want to learn before the test, record what happened and let the result change the next version. If every objection becomes proof that others do not understand your vision, you have made the idea impossible to improve.

The personal side belongs in [thanking the person who believed first](/writing/thank-the-person-who-believed-in-you-first/). This article adds the practical next step: give the belief contact with something observable.

You do not need the whole world to agree before you begin. You do need to listen when the world answers.
