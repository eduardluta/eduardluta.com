The video lasts about two seconds. A child smiles with an ice cream in hand, and the words above the scene look far into the future: I hope this is one of the moments that stays.[1]

That is the feeling I wanted to share. The caption uses “last breath” language, but I mean it as a wish about what matters, not a prediction about how anyone's memory works at the end of life.

The scene is small enough to pass without a record. There is no ceremony, speech or major achievement. There is an ice cream and an expression I wanted to keep.

## An ordinary day can become interesting later

We usually know why we save the big occasions. A short clip from an unremarkable day can be harder to justify until time has passed and the ordinary details have changed.

In four studies published in 2014, Ting Zhang and colleagues found that people underestimated the later pleasure of rediscovering ordinary experiences.[2] They did not study final memories.

That research gives me a modest reason to value the little recording. What seems too familiar to explain today may be exactly what I am glad to see again later.

A date, a little context and a short sentence can make a saved clip easier to understand years later. These are editorial suggestions. They do not promise that recording a moment will preserve every feeling attached to it.

## Keep the moment without making it perform

I do not want every good experience to become a production. Sometimes a tiny record is enough, and sometimes being there matters more than getting the frame.

This is also why I would leave the scene simple. The clip does not need an invented account of where we were going or what happened before it. The smile already gives it a reason to exist.

In [small moments with children](/writing/small-moments-with-children/), I wrote about another brief everyday scene. Here, the thought is about returning to the record later: a small gift from an ordinary day to a future version of ourselves.

**I cannot know what will stay. I can know what I am glad to have noticed.**
