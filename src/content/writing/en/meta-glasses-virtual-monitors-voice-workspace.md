We are walking through the trees when the conversation turns into a very different room: one with screens everywhere and no monitor on the desk. My companion wants the glasses to become his display. I am interested in what happens after that—when you can explain what you want and get on with creating it.

That is the idea behind this video. It is a conversation about a possible way of working, not a demonstration that every pair of Meta glasses can run your office.

**Can Meta glasses replace a monitor? Some headset setups can provide virtual computer screens today. The answer changes completely with the product you mean.**

## Four products hiding behind one phrase

The familiar [Ray-Ban Meta camera glasses](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/09/introducing-orion-our-first-true-augmented-reality-glasses/) have no display. They cannot put a spreadsheet in front of your eyes.

[Meta Ray-Ban Display](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/09/meta-ray-ban-display-ai-glasses-emg-wristband/) adds a small screen for things such as messages, directions and captions. That is a different job from arranging a laptop's windows around a room. Meta's [September 2026 update](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/new-features-for-meta-ray-ban-display-navigation-hologram/) announces German availability from **13 October 2026**; on this article's publication date, that is still ahead.

The newly announced [Meta VR Glasses](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-meta-vr-glasses-3d-movies-immersive-live-sports-100-grams/) are closer to the workspace idea: multiple screens, with voice and gesture controls. Meta says spring 2027, at an announced US price of $1,299.99. The roughly 100-gram glasses connect to a separate computing and battery puck. These are manufacturer claims about an upcoming product, not my test results or a German retail offer.

I would not choose a device from the phrase “AI glasses” alone. I would first decide whether I want to capture something, glance at information, or spend an afternoon working in it.

## The virtual-monitor route that already exists

For a supported Windows PC, [Microsoft Mixed Reality Link](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/experience/compatibility/about-windows-mixed-reality-link) connects Quest 3 or 3S with Windows 11, version 22H2 or later. The computer and headset need the same network; Microsoft's instructions specify 5 or 6 GHz Wi-Fi. This is a computer connection, not the disappearance of the computer.

[Meta's Virtual Display guide](https://www.meta.com/en-gb/help/quest/1370025034331518/) allows up to three virtual displays in this setup. It lists a 16:9 layout and a default 1080p resolution. That gives us a concrete way to understand the space, without guessing how much faster someone would work.

One 1920 × 1080 desktop contains **2,073,600 logical pixels**. Two contain 4,147,200; three contain 6,220,800. Three equal screens offer three times the desktop area, or **200% more than one**.

Those are coordinates available for windows. They are not the physical pixel count of the headset's panels, and they do not mean three screens are equally sharp or visible at once. Nor does three times the area mean three times the output.

For me, the useful arrangement would be simple: the thing I am making in the middle, its source material to one side, and a preview on the other. If I spend the session hunting for windows, the larger room has not helped.

## Voice changes the input; judgment stays with you

The conversation moves from monitors to speaking instructions, then to the idea of creating directly from thought. I read that last part as the ambition: less friction between an idea and something real. The clip does not show a device reading thoughts.

Even the [Neural Band used with Meta Ray-Ban Display](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/09/meta-ray-ban-display-ai-glasses-emg-wristband/) detects muscle signals at the wrist. Its name should not turn a gesture interface into a claim about mind reading.

A practical voice workflow still has three parts. Explain the result you want. Inspect what appeared. Correct the part that missed your intention. “Make this clearer” can start an edit; someone still has to notice whether the meaning survived it.

Before replacing my desk, I would try an ordinary task: read a dense page, edit a paragraph and check a small table. Then I would dictate a change and correct a mistake. Can I read comfortably? Find the right window? Get a precise result? Those answers matter more to me than the size of the virtual room.

In [my earlier video about glasses, a watch and capturing ideas](/writing/meta-glasses-apple-watch-capturing-creative-ideas/), the question was how to keep a thought before it disappears. Here it is what happens next: giving that thought enough space to become work.

**The exciting part is the shorter distance from an idea to something you can use. The monitor is only one piece of that.**
