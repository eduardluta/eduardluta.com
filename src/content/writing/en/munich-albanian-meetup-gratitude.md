The car door is open. It is dark outside. I get out and break into a little dance.

That is the video. A playful edit, a burst of energy, and an on-screen line about coming back from meeting dua.com users and having had a great time. In the caption, I thank Munich for the pleasure of meeting so many Albanians.

Sometimes seven seconds are enough to say what a long explanation would flatten: **I am grateful we met.**

## The people behind an app

A profile belongs to a person. A name on a screen belongs to somebody with a life that continues after they close it. Meeting people in person brings that back into view.

That is what I want this little clip to keep. The pleasure of the encounter. The feeling of coming back with more energy than you left with. The reason an adventure like this is worth doing.

This is a thank-you, not an attendance report. There is no verified headcount or named venue in the post, and seven seconds cannot stand in for the whole Albanian community in Munich.

## Let the next step stay personal

After a good evening, the easiest thing is to let the feeling become another item in the camera roll. I would rather give it one small next step.

Remember something specific from a conversation. If you exchanged contact details and staying in touch was welcome, send a real thank-you. Suggest an activity you would actually enjoy together. An ordinary coffee counts; the next meeting does not have to become an event.

## A local thread worth following

For readers who want to explore Albanian language beyond a single meeting, [LMU Munich's Albanology department](https://www.albanologie.uni-muenchen.de/interesse-am-studium/albanisch-studieren/index.html) publishes study and language-course options. Its Albanian I–IV sequence spans four semesters; the introductory course normally begins in winter semester. Check current admission and guest-student arrangements with the university before planning to attend.

That is a language-learning route, not a claim that the university organised this meetup. It is one concrete place to continue an interest in Albanian life in Munich.

I wrote about a similar pleasure in [meeting our people in Hamburg](/writing/from-hamburg-with-love-meeting-our-people/). Different city, same reason to get out of the car and meet people.

Munich, thank you for this part of the adventure.
